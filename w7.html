Srinivasa Ramanujan

Mathematical Genius of India

About

Srinivasa Ramanujan was born on December 22, 1887, in Erode, Tamil Nadu, India. He was a self-taught mathematician who made extraordinary contributions to mathematical analysis, number theory, infinite series, and continued fractions. Despite having little formal training, his work amazed mathematicians worldwide.

Contributions

  • Developed groundbreaking formulas in number theory
  • Worked on infinite series and continued fractions
  • Collaborated with G.H. Hardy at Cambridge University
  • Discovered Ramanujan Prime and Ramanujan Theta functions

Legacy

Ramanujan’s work continues to influence modern mathematics and research. His life story inspires students across the world. National Mathematics Day is celebrated in India on his birthday to honor his contributions.
